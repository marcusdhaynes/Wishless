# Wishless — Accepted Design Decisions

These are design requirements, NOT claims that gameplay systems have been implemented.

- Dark fairy-tale world, gothic pencil-sketch concept art, purple-and-gold palette.
- Real-time combat with optional tactical pause.
- Drop-in/out co-op, up to four participants or AI companions; host story remains canonical.
- Fixed major landmarks; procedural surroundings where appropriate.
- Low RAM, small install, smooth performance on modest PCs.
- PC, Nintendo, PlayStation, Xbox targeted; Android and iOS conditional.

Each future update must clearly distinguish **accepted**, **proposed**, **implemented**, and **verified** decisions.
